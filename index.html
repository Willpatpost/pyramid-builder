PYRAMID BUILDER

Mine the desert. Survive the night. Raise a monument.

How to Play

Goal

Build a solid stepped pyramid out of sandstone with a 15×15 base (8 layers: 15, 13, 11 … 1 — 680 blocks). Every block of every layer must be sandstone.

Controls

  • W A S D — move  |  Space — jump  |  Shift — sneak
  • Mouse — look  |  Left click (hold) — mine / attack
  • Right click — place block / eat dates
  • 1–9 / wheel — select hotbar slot  |  E — inventory & crafting
  • Esc — pause

Survival

  • Sandstone hides in a band under the dunes — dig a stairway and mine it with a pickaxe, or craft it from 4 sand.
  • Palms near the oasis give wood: planks, sticks, tools, swords.
  • At night mummies rise from the sand. Fight them with a sword or wall yourself in. They drop sand.
  • Breaking palm leaves sometimes drops dates — eat them to heal.
  • Death drops your inventory where you fell. Respawn and run it back.
